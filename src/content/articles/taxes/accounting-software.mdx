For most English-speaking freelancers, **Accountable** or **Norman** is the easiest choice: both work in English, file your VAT returns and prepare your <Term de="Anlage EÜR" /> and income tax return. If you are a <Term de="Kleinunternehmer" /> and don't charge VAT, the yearly plans from Norman or Sorted, about €100 a year, are enough. If you'd rather not do any of it yourself, Kontist's tax service hands everything to a tax advisor for a fixed monthly fee.

## What accounting software does for a freelancer

As a freelancer in Germany you need to do four things regularly, and good software covers all of them:

1. **Write invoices** that meet German rules, including e-invoices (<Term de="E-Rechnung" />).
2. **Collect receipts** and match them to your bank transactions.
3. **File the VAT return** (<Term de="Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung" />) every month or quarter, if you charge VAT.
4. **Prepare the yearly returns:** the Anlage EÜR, the income tax return and, if you charge VAT, the annual VAT return.

Cheaper plans usually stop after step 2. Check that the plan you pick covers the steps you actually need, and that it sends returns directly to <Term de="ELSTER" />, the tax office's portal.

<Callout kind="note" title="E-invoices">
Since 2025, every business in Germany must be able to receive e-invoices. Sending them to German business clients becomes mandatory in 2027 if your revenue was above €800,000 the year before, and in 2028 for everyone else. Kleinunternehmer are exempt from sending them. Accountable, Norman and sevdesk create e-invoices in the XRechnung and ZUGFeRD formats. Sorted and Kontist don't mention e-invoices on their websites, so ask before you sign up. See [how to write a German invoice](/taxes/invoice).
</Callout>

## Accountable

Accountable is built for freelancers who want to do their own taxes, but with guidance. The app walks you through each return in plain language, and the Taxes plan includes a chat with tax coaches.

- **Free:** invoices and e-invoices in the mobile app, with 5 receipts a month.
- **Invoicing Plus, €9.90 a month:** invoicing on the web as well as on mobile.
- **Bookkeeping, €19.90 a month:** unlimited receipts, up to 5 bank accounts, and the yearly returns for Kleinunternehmer.
- **Taxes, €29.90 a month:** adds VAT returns and all yearly returns for VAT-registered freelancers, including the income tax return.
- Monthly billing costs more: €12.40, €24.90 and €37.40.

**Good to know:** the Taxes plan includes a guarantee on its tax filings of up to €500 per filing. The free plan has no web access.

## Norman

Norman works fully in English and has the most generous free plan: unlimited invoices, e-invoices and bookkeeping, with no time limit.

- **Pro, €9 a month billed yearly (€11 monthly):** bank sync, automatic receipt matching, DATEV export for your tax advisor.
- **Max, €29 a month billed yearly (€35 monthly):** VAT returns and EU sales lists, plus the Anlage EÜR, income tax and trade tax returns, filed directly with the tax office.
- **Kleinunternehmer, €99 a year:** every return for one tax year, from VAT to income tax.

**Good to know:** on Max, the yearly returns are only included with yearly billing, and one paid year covers one tax year. The Kleinunternehmer plan is billed yearly only.

## sevdesk

sevdesk is one of Germany's best-known accounting tools, and the whole interface can be switched to English. It is strong on bookkeeping and VAT returns, and it prepares your Anlage EÜR. Its plans don't include the income tax return, so you file that yourself or with a tax advisor.

- **Free:** up to 3 invoices or e-invoices a month.
- **Accounting, €25.90 a month billed monthly:** receipt scanning, bank connections, VAT returns sent through ELSTER, and the Anlage EÜR. Terms of 12 or 24 months cost less per month.

**Good to know:** sevdesk often runs steep discounts, so check the current price before you sign up. It suits freelancers who use a tax advisor for the yearly returns, since it exports in the DATEV format. [Visit sevdesk](https://sevdesk.de)

## Sorted

Sorted is a lean, low-cost option that files directly with the tax office. The app works in English and German, and its tax advisors answer in English.

- **Starter, free:** registering with the tax office, invoices, income and expense tracking, bank connection.
- **Kleinunternehmer, €98 a year:** Anlage EÜR and income tax return, for income under €22,000 a year.
- **Pro, €20 a month (billed quarterly):** VAT returns, annual VAT return, Anlage EÜR, income tax return.
- **Tax advisor on demand:** a question from €50, a review of your returns from €120.

**Good to know:** the Kleinunternehmer plan is limited to income under €22,000 a year, the scheme's old limit. Since 2025, the scheme itself allows up to €25,000 of revenue, so check the plan still fits if you are close. Sorted doesn't mention structured e-invoices; a comparison by Norman, a competitor, says it creates PDF invoices only. [Visit Sorted](https://en.getsorted.de)

## Kontist tax service

Kontist is a business bank with a done-for-you option: Kontist Steuerberatungsgesellschaft, a tax advisory company in the Kontist group, does your bookkeeping, VAT returns, Anlage EÜR and income tax return for a fixed €179 a month plus VAT.

- **Included:** monthly bookkeeping, VAT returns, and the yearly business and private tax returns.
- **Requires:** a Kontist Premium business account as your main business account, which is included in the price.
- **Only for:** solo self-employed people registered in Germany, such as freelancers and Kleinunternehmer, with no or very few cash payments, no online sales of physical goods, and no duty to keep double-entry books. It is not available for companies such as a GmbH or UG, or for partnerships such as a GbR. Kontist can also exclude some professions, including craftspeople, cleaners, artists and restaurant owners.

**Good to know:** if you start mid-year, the monthly fees for the earlier months of that year are charged too. [Visit Kontist](https://kontist.com/en/)

## Which one fits you

- **You are a Kleinunternehmer and don't charge VAT:** Norman's Kleinunternehmer plan or Sorted's Kleinunternehmer plan, both around €100 a year. Read more about the [Kleinunternehmer rules](/taxes/kleinunternehmer).
- **You charge VAT and want to file yourself, in English:** Accountable Taxes or Norman Max. Accountable gives you more guidance, Norman costs a little less.
- **You already have a tax advisor:** sevdesk Accounting or Norman Pro for bookkeeping and VAT returns, with a DATEV export for your <Term de="Steuerberater" />.
- **You want someone else to do it:** Kontist's tax service, or a tax advisor of your own. Compare the costs in [Do you need a tax advisor?](/taxes/tax-advisor)
- **Your finances are very simple:** a spreadsheet and ELSTER are free. See [VAT returns explained](/taxes/vat-returns) and [taxes for freelancers](/taxes/freelancer-taxes).

## German-only alternatives

If you are comfortable working in German, Lexware Office, WISO MeinBüro and Papierkram are also popular with freelancers. Their interfaces are in German only, although all three can produce invoices in English, so they are not covered in detail here.

## Costs and taxes

Prices for business software are usually shown without VAT. If you charge VAT, you pay the net price in the end, because you reclaim the <Term de="Vorsteuer" />. As a Kleinunternehmer you pay 19% on top. Either way, the subscription is a business expense: see [deductible expenses for freelancers](/taxes/deductible-expenses-for-freelancers).
